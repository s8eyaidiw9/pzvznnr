# The Rock and Dany Garcia Are Going to Throw a Fitness Lollapalooza in October

Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson and Dany Garcia have announced their newest venture, Athleticon.

The fitness and entertainment event will include panels and competitions.

Athleticon will take place in Atlanta in October.

Pop culture fans have Comic Con, and Stranger Things fans have Stranger Con, but if you're a health and wellness buff, there really isn't a convention that you can go to—until now. Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson and his business partner Dany Garcia have recently announced the upcoming arrival of the fitness event Athleticon, and it sounds amazing.

Athleticon will include competitions, panels, live performances, and exclusive premieres, and you can also expect to explore facets of nutrition, healthy living, sport tech and fitness at the event.

Athleticon will focus on a combination of athletics, wellness, and entertainment, and each aspect of the two-day event will feature special guests.
