5. Safety Concerns With Absorption

Topical anesthetics are absorbed through the skin. When overused, especially on broken or sensitive skin, they can enter the bloodstream.

Medical experts have raised concerns about:

Irregular heartbeat

Dizziness or confusion

Nausea

In rare cases, serious systemic reactions

These risks are higher with:

Prescription-strength products used without guidance

Combining multiple numbing products

Using them with alcohol or recreational drugs
