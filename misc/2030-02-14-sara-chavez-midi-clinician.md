Sara Chavez, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

Rooted in primary care: "I began my healthcare career in 2012, and before joining Midi, I worked as a primary care provider in family medicine. There, I cared for patients across all stages of life. Over time, I developed a strong interest in focused women’s health care, where personalized treatment can create meaningful improvements in quality of life."

Improving everyday health: "Some of the most rewarding moments in my work come when I see women achieve real, lasting change such as successful weight management and improved diabetes control. These results not only enhance physical health but also restore confidence and overall well-being."

Individualized, patient-centered care: "I believe the foundation of effective care is listening closely and tailoring each plan to the individual. At Midi, I’m inspired by the opportunity to help women in midlife feel their best and live healthier, more balanced lives. Seeing the difference that personalized care can make, both physically and emotionally, is what motivates me every day."
