# Ultimate Beastmaster Wants to Be the Fittest Show on Netflix

That’s “The Beast.” In Ultimate Beastmaster, 108 fitness freaks will run said obstacle course, and the winner will advance to an international competition, where they will meet the winners from eight other Beastmaster competitions to become—you guessed it—the Ultimate Beastmaster.
