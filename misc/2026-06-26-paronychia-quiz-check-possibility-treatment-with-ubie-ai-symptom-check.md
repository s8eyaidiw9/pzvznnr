What is Paronychia?

Inflammation of the skin around the nails, commonly caused by bacterial or fungal infections that enter through broken skin (e.g., from hangnails or nail-biting).

Typical Symptoms of Paronychia

Fingers or toes red, swollen, and stiff

Pain in the nails

Finger pain

Toe pain

Swelling in the affected area

Pus-filled blisters or bumps on the skin

Change in nail color

Redness, bumps, or irritation on the skin

Diagnostic Questions for Paronychia

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are your fingers or toes red, swollen, and stiff?

Are you experiencing pain in your nails?

Are you experiencing pain in your finger?

Are you experiencing toe pain?

Do you have swelling in the affected area?

Treatment of Paronychia

For simple cases, warm soaks and antibiotic cream or tablets can treat the infection. However, more serious cases may require a small procedure to drain pus from the infection.
