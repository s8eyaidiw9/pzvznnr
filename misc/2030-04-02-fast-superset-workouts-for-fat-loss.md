Q: What are some of the basic supersets that you can do or that you incorporate a workout?

CB: One of the most basic ones is dumbbell squat and then a push up variation.

This can include the dumbbell squat. Which is an under rated exercise because it uses all of your muscle groups as well as your upper back a lot more than most people consider. And then pair that with an elevated push up.

An elevated push up is just one of the dozens of push up variations that I use. But in this one you put your hand up on a four to six inch block, just one hand. And the other hand is flat on the floor.

And that exercise works a muscle group called the serratus anterior, in addition to all the push up muscles. The serratus anterior, if you’re familiar with body builders, when they do their abdominal poses and you see those finger like muscles coming down over the lateral side of the rib cage there.

That’s the serratus anterior. And if you do a couple of sets of those, you’ll have soreness in the area you’re not used to the next day.

So, that’s a simple superset workout for fat loss. A lower body exercise paired with a pushup variation.

Another one is a dumbbell chest press and an inverted row. That’s a great one because now you don’t have to use your dumbbells for pressing and rowing.

One other one, just while we’re at it is dumbbell RDL A dumbbell Romanian dead lift and decline pushup. Decline pushups are a little tougher than normal pushups. And that’s another big combination right there.
