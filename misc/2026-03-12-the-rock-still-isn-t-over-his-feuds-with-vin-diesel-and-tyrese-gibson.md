# The Rock Still Isn't Over His Feuds With Vin Diesel and Tyrese Gibson

It’s well-known that Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson and his Fast and Furious co-stars have had their differences. The feuding seemed to have died down by the end of 2017 — but The Rock still has some feelings to share.

Rolling Stone released a wide-ranging interview (and this follow-up) with Johnson that not only addressed his workouts and his personal successes, but also the months-long drama between him, Vin Diesel, and Tyrese Gibson. And Johnson didn't exactly hold back.

The Rock on Vin Diesel

[With Vin Diesel and](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/blog/0245672956.html)

The Rock and Vin Diesel's feud reportedly began when the former may or may not have called the latter a "candy-ass" on Facebook. Their beef was apparently so bad, they had a hard time promoting Fate of the Furious together. In April of 2017, they finally seemed to put their drama behind them.

Here's what John told Rolling Stone about Vin Diesel — seems polite, until you get to the part at the end:

Johnson says their beef came down to a disagreement about professionalism. "Vin and I had a few discussions, including an important face-to-face in my trailer," he says. "And what I came to realize is that we have a fundamental difference in philosophies on how we approach moviemaking and collaborating. It took me some time, but I'm grateful for that clarity. Whether we work together again or not."

[Feuds With](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/blog/9399651578.html)

Does that mean he might not be back for the ninth installment? "I'm not quite sure," he says. "Right now I'm concentrating on making the spinoff as good as it can be" – Hobbs and Shaw, co-starring Jason Statham, due next year. "But I wish him all the best, and I harbor no ill will there, just because of the clarity we have." He considers this, then lets out a big, sly laugh. "Actually, you can erase that last part about 'no ill will.' We'll just keep it with the clarity."

The Rock on Tyrese Gibson

[Feuds With Vin Diesel](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Gibson seemed to get seriously upset when it was announced that Johnson’s Fast and Furious character, Hobbs, was getting his own spin-off. That spin-off, now in production, pushed back the release date of the next Fast and Furious film. (Here's a full timeline of The Rock and Gibson's feud, from Gibson's fiery Instagram posts to when Diesel stepped in to defend Johnson.)

Here's what Johnson had to say about Gibson:

[Rock Still](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/blog/9314729123.html)

Actor Tyrese Gibson, who plays Roman in the Fast movies, has been particularly vocal in calling out Johnson on social media for being selfish and putting himself before the franchise. But Johnson doesn't consider what they have a beef. "A beef requires two people," he says. "Tyrese, for reasons unbeknownst to me and unbeknownst to a lot of people, went off in his own direction, down a path that was never understood. I never heard from him once – he never called, never texted. So I honestly didn't have a beef, because I just got to a point where I didn't care."

[and Tyrese Gibson](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/)

Johnson said his co-stars' anger over Hobbs and Shaw was "so silly." "At the end of the day, it's smart business to expand on the franchise and build it out," he said.

[Rock Still](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/blog/7035586013.html)

[Feuds With Vin](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/3087243325.html)

While it may not be the smartest thing to discuss a fight that was already dead and buried, Johnson said he thinks the spinoff is actually good business for everyone:

"The analogy that was given to me, which was very funny, was that it's like if Robert Downey. Jr., was pissed about Captain America and these other movies. Let it all happen! Let it all grow. It helps out everybody. Regardless of a few who don't want it to happen, for whatever reasons, I know that big picture, long-term, a buildout is the best thing for everybody. So you can bitch, you can moan, you can complain. But the train is leaving the station, and it's not going to stop. So be smart, get on the train, think about the big picture – and let's create multiple trains."

Luckily for all those involved in the movie franchise, Fast and Furious 9 is still on track for a 2020 release date, which will be here sooner than you think.
