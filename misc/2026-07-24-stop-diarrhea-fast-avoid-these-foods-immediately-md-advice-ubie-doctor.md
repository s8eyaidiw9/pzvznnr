Which foods should be avoided when experiencing diarrhea?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

When experiencing diarrhea, avoid foods high in histamines, such as aged cheeses, fermented foods, cured meats, and alcohol, as these can worsen symptoms. Also steer clear of spicy foods, fatty or fried foods, dairy, caffeine, and artificial sweeteners, which can irritate your digestive system and prolong discomfort. Instead, focus on bland, easy-to-digest options like bananas, rice, applesauce, and toast (the BRAT diet).

Because diarrhea can stem from many causes—ranging from mild food sensitivities to infections or chronic conditions—identifying the root cause is essential for effective relief.
