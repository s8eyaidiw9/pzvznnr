Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, you should talk to your doctor about GLP-1 medications and muscle loss. Research indicates that GLP-1 receptor agonists—such as semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound)—can lead to reductions in lean muscle mass alongside fat loss during rapid weight reduction. Preserving muscle matters because it supports metabolism, mobility, strength, and long-term health outcomes. Your doctor can assess your risk, recommend resistance training, ensure adequate protein intake, and monitor body composition to help you maintain muscle while benefiting from the medication.

If you're experiencing symptoms like unexplained weakness, fatigue, or changes in body composition while on a GLP-1, don't wait to investigate. GLP-1 medications are used to help control blood sugar levels in people with diabetes. Some studies have looked into how these drugs might affect muscles. One study found that GLP-1 medications could be linked to muscle problems like rhabdomyolysis, which is a serious condition where muscles break down. Another study suggested that these drugs might impact muscle health, especially in people who have conditions like sarcopenia, which is when muscles become weaker with age.

Why Talk to Your Doctor?

Muscle Health: Since GLP-1 medications might affect muscles, it is important to discuss any concerns with your doctor.
