# Jack Black Shaved All His Hair Off and Looks Years Younger

Multiple celebrities have changed up their looks in quarantine, but Jack Black shaving his quarantine hair on YouTube may be one of the biggest switches.

[His Hair](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/blog/0812344810.html)

The actor took to his Gablinski Games YouTube channel to do the deed, and well, apparently people really wanted to see it. So far, the YouTube video has garnered a whopping 3.1 million views. The footage is surprisingly engaging.

[Hair Off and](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/)

Black begins the process in a state akin to Robin Williams' character at the beginning of the original Jumanji (coincidence or no given Black's role in the reboots?). The actor looks determinedly into the camera, removes his glasses, and grabs a pair of kitchen scissors. Yes, he does in fact use kitchen scissors to do most of the dirty work. As he grabs a chunk of beard hair at the front of his face and begins slicing, viewers can hear the steady saw of the shears as they attempt to cut through the thick hair.

[Shaved All His Hair](https://insulin-pathways.hbsnzj.com/blog/7862204063.html)

After taking out most of his beard, he moves on to his shoulder length hair, shoring it above his ears and at one point giving himself a mullet that might make Billy Ray Cyrus jealous.

Finally, it's time for the clippers. Black takes out every stitch of his beard seeming to age backwards, according to fan comments. Then, he moves on to his hair. Near the end of the massive transformation, Black's son comes out smiling as he approaches his dad causing the actor to laugh.

In the final moments of the video, Black's son helps him take out the last few bits of his hair, and the actor looks in the camera proclaiming, "And that's it. It's as simple as that. That's how you get rid of your Covid hairs."
