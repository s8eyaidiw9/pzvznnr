Eye twitching in women can be caused by deficiencies in key vitamins and minerals, most commonly magnesium and vitamin B12. Low levels of these nutrients disrupt normal nerve and muscle function, leading to involuntary eyelid spasms (myokymia). Other contributors include low vitamin D, calcium, and potassium, as well as dehydration, stress, and caffeine.

Explanation

Eye twitching can be annoying and sometimes is linked to vitamin or mineral deficiencies. Magnesium is a mineral that helps with many body functions, and a lack of it can affect the eyes, as seen in studies on optic nerve health. Vitamin B12 is another important nutrient, and not having enough of it can lead to involuntary movements, including eye twitching.

Here are some key points:

Magnesium Deficiency: A lack of magnesium, known as hypomagnesemia, can influence the optic nerve, leading to issues like eye twitching.
