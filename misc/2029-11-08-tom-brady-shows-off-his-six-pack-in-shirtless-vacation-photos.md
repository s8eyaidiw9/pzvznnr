# Tom Brady Shows Off His Six-Pack in Shirtless Vacation Photos

Tom Brady might not be playing with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers any more, but he's still soaking up that Florida sun. The seven-time Super Bowl champion recently posted a carousel of photos to Instagram of himself and his kids out on the water near his home in Indian Creek, Miami.

Sporting swimwear and a snorkel, Brady's lean and toned, post-NFL physique was on full display—and he's unapologetic about posting thirst traps on main over the holidays.

"I did Michigan and Massachusetts winters for a long time," he joked in the caption. "Let me have this."

Brady has been living his best life since announcing his (second) retirement in February 2023, traveling and spending quality time with his children Jack, Benny and Vivi, whom he co-parents with ex-wife Gisele Bündchen. And now that he's not locked into an intense training regiment, he's had the time and energy to invest in his own mental wellbeing in addition to his physical health.

"I’m down about 10 pounds, but I’m actually very fit right now," he said during an appearance on Larry Fitzgerald and Jim Gray's Let's Go podcast in September. "I haven’t had the stress that I had while I was playing so that’s allowed me to focus a little bit more on my physical health... You could prioritize a lot of other things—career, kids, relationships, family, greater good, community—but at the end of the day, physical and mental health should sit at the top of the pyramid."
