When is the Super Bowl?

The game will kick off on Sunday, February 7 at 6:30 p.m. ET in Raymond James Stadium in Tampa, Florida, making the Buccaneers the first team in NFL history to play the Super Bowl in their home stadium. Pre-game festivities will surely be going on for hours beforehand as well.

Where can I watch the Super Bowl?

The Super Bowl is set to broadcast live on the CBS network, which also makes it super easy for those who decided to cut the cord and don’t have cable for the game. Anyone can stream the game for free on CBSSports.com or using the CBS Sports app (available for iOS and Android).
