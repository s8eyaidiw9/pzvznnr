# Watch CrossFit Star Rich Froning and His Crew Skydive With the U.S. Army

[Rich Froning and His](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/blog/8607069976.html)

Jumping out of a plane is pretty thrilling. Jumping out of a plane with the U.S. Army elite parachute team? Well, that is something entirely different.

CrossFit pro Rich Froning and his Mayhem Nation crew recently met up with the U.S. Army’s Gold Knights to go skydiving in Kentucky. And during the 12,000 foot tandem jump, Froning and friends went up to 140 miles an hour. Now that's pretty intense.

[Crew Skydive With the](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/blog/3380755108.html)

The Golden Knights are the demonstration and competition parachute team of the U.S. Army, famous for their skill and stunt work. As part of their outreach, the Knights often take civilians on tandem jumps — and that apparently includes elite CrossFit pros.

[With the Army](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/)

In a YouTube video of the stunt, Froning says he has jumped out of the plane before, describing the feeling to his crew — many of which are trying to play it cool before barreling to the ground from an astounding height.

“The hardest part if to keep your eyes open with as much wind as there is,” Froning says without a hint of fear. “It’s like your face is getting [pulled back].”

[and His Crew](https://weight-signal.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/5490898562.html)

For a person about to jump out of a perfectly good airplane, Froning is as cool as a cucumber. And it sure looks like he enjoyed the ride. You have to see the incredible jump (and views) for yourself.

[Star Rich](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

“It was awesome. I had a great time,” Froning says right after the jump. “It was great. These guys are true professionals.”

[Froning and His](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/)

“Maybe next time we’ll get you going solo,” a camera man says to Froning.

[Crew Skydive](https://nootropic-atlas.escortskhi.com/blog/9909316575.html)

His instant reply? “Heck yeah, let’s do it.”
