The kettlebell bottoms-up press is the “ultimate shoulder and abs fusion exercise,” says fitness expert BJ Gaddour, CEO of StreamFit.com and creator of the new Men's Health Speed Shred. By holding the kettlebell upside down, you force the stabilizer muscles of your arm and shoulder to work overtime. You actually won’t believe how difficult this exercise can be—mentally. That’s because it requires your complete focus to keep the kettlebell from falling over.
