# This Lat Pulldown Variation Belongs In Your Upper-Body Workout

The kneeling lat pulldown is one of the most underrated exercises out there, and it happens to be a favorite of Men's Health fitness editor, Ebenezer Samuel.

[Upper-Body Workout](https://cellular.wiki/ayle93p/)

This exercise works a ton of muscles, like the ones in your back, shoulders, and arms, and should be a regular addition to your upper-body days.

For newbies, learning proper form is important to reduce your risk of injury, and the kneeling lat pulldown is the perfect way to start. One major tip Samuel recommends that you should keep in mind while doing this exercise is to squeeze your glutes and tighten your abs when you get into position. This ensures your position is stable. When you go to pull the bar down, make sure to continue squeezing your glutes and abs. Watch the video above to see exactly how Samuel does it.

When you get used to doing this exercise, you can include variations like close-grip and wide-grip lat pulldowns to target different muscles even further.
