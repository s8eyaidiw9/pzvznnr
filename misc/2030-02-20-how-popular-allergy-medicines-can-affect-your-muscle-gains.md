But don’t be too concerned just yet: The study used high doses of two types of antihistamines together. Most people take only one drug, with a half or even a third of the dosage taken by the study participants, says Halliwill.

So if you’re taking the recommended dose of one type of antihistamine for short-term use—say, a few days or even weeks to treat seasonal hay fever or occasional heartburn—you don’t need to worry about it affecting your gains.

Still, more research regarding muscle recovery needs to be done on the effects of taking antihistamines—even in the normal dosages—on a daily basis for months at a time, he says.
