Science.bio Has Permanently Closed

Science.bio announced its permanent closure, stating: “It is with a heavy heart that we announce Science.bio will be permanently closing. All outstanding orders will be either fulfilled or fully refunded.”

The company’s official statement confirmed that after careful consideration, Science.bio made the decision to permanently close and discontinue the sale of all research products, expressing gratitude for customer support over the years.

This marks the end of a company that had one of the most turbulent histories in the research chemical space: shutting down once in February 2022, relaunching in May 2023, and now closing permanently in early 2026.

Why Did Science.bio Close?

The closure was not triggered by a specific enforcement action. It was a business decision made in response to a convergence of pressures that also led other major vendors to shut down around the same time.

Who Was Science.bio?

Science.bio, originally known as IRC.bio before federal regulations forced its first closure in November 2018. relaunched in April 2019 and became one of the most trusted names in the research compound space. The company offered over 200 products including SARMs, nootropics, peptides, metabolics, and longevity compounds, serving clients ranging from individual lab technicians to larger research institutions.

The company was especially well known for:

Quality Control: Strict batch and lot tracking, third-party testing via HPLC, NMR spectroscopy, TLC, ICP-MS, GC-MS, and other methods, with full lab reports publicly available for every product.

Product Range: SARMs including Ostarine MK-2866, Testolone RAD-140, Ligandrol LGD-4033, Andarine S4, and YK-11, alongside a broad nootropic and peptide catalog.

Transparency: All compounds were manufactured in ISO-compliant facilities, with products traceable by lot number to third-party certificates of analysis.

Shipping: Free 2–3 day FedEx domestic shipping, with signature confirmation on orders over $200 and reshipping guarantees for customs issues.

The company held a Trustpilot rating of 4.5 stars, with over 87% of reviewers rating their experience as excellent.
