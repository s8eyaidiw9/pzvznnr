Few things scream “I'm a fit guy” quite like a pair of strong, jacked biceps. And the key to building a set of sleeve-busters is an arm routine that gives you a great muscle pump.

To score that essential pump, you have to be sure to keep the weight moderate and reps high, says Marfred Suazo, C.S.C.S., co-owner of Superiior Fitness in New York City. If you pick a weight that’s too heavy for your biceps exercises, your forearms take over the bulk of the work. “Your wrist flexors and forearms get a lot more contraction because you’re gripping the weight harder,” Suazo says.

Ready to boost your biceps? Try Suazo’s four-move, muscle-building routine. Complete all the sets of each exercise before moving on to the next.
