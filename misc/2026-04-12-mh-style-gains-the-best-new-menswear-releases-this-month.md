Welcome to MH Style Gains, where Men’s Health's Style Team finds the best new menswear releases of the month so you can look and feel your best.

SUMMER IS BASICALLY here, and the menswear drops are heating up. In fact, our fashion director Ted Stafford and assistant fashion market editor Imani Granger have combed through their favorite releases this month, which span just about every dress code. Mango's new suiting collab with Boglioli is chockfull of (relatively) affordable, high-quality formalwear, which comes just in time for wedding season. As for smart-casual looks, Levi's released is a warm-weather version of its iconic 511, and Seiko's SRPG35 field watch looks great with just about anything in your closet. Of course, this being summer, we're all about dressing for leisure, and some of our favorite menswear brands like Todd Snyder and Sperry have you covered with a vacation-friendly boat shoe. Salomon's new futuristic trail sneaker can get you through more rugged adventures, and Prince's new Target collection is all about acing Pickleball fits. Read on for our favorite menswear releases this month.

Spanish giant Mango teamed up with luxe tailor Boglioli for a sharp collection of formalwear that you can dress up or down. Take this double-breasted blazer, which looks just as dapper with its matching wool suit pant, or with a light-wash jean. That versatility is thanks to its sleek design, which is fitted but not tight, and the notch lapels and welt pockets that add clean lines and a visual interest for a smart (but not stuffy) vibe. Our fashion team says it's a failsafe uniform to wear to the office, or your next summer wedding.

Salomon ACS + FT Sneaker

[New Menswear](https://allpepay.com/blog/6906387024.html)

Trail sneaker king Salomon beefed up its classic shoe with a Blade Runner-like design that's still rugged enough of for your weekend hikes. See: its grippy outsole and sturdy upper that can help keep your feet in place. Though it's built for the outdoors, we think the ACS + FT sneaker looks just as great in the city, especially with our Gorpcore fits. "It looks great for travel, the gym, and everywhere in between," says Stafford.

Seiko SRPG35 Watch

Automatic watches are a dime a dozen. But Seiko's ticker stands out from the pack for its retro rugged style that looks as great as it performs. We like how the beige nylon strap contrasts with the bold black dial, so you can mix and match with all sorts of looks. This being a field watch, it has a sporty steel case that can take a licking, and has ample water resistance to handle the occasional wipeout. "It's a quality timepiece that you absolutely need in your Summer watch rotation," Stafford says.

Filson Duffle Bag

Most travel bags break down after enough mileage. Not this Filson duffel. Our fashion team has field tested this and found it manages to look better with time. That Benjamin Buttoning is thanks to its waxed cotton fabric, which ages beautifully, and the handsome leather handle that gets even more comfortable after each journey. Plus, this is Filson, so you know it's built to last. "It's a brand that prides itself on its quality, and this orange color will assure you and your bag stand out together," Stafford says.

Levi's 511™ Performance Pants

Levi's 511 is a jean meant to be worn year round. Case in point: the jean giant dropped a summer-ready version of the iconic style. It's made with a lighter (but still durable!) weave that's more breathable than your standard denim, and has a bit of elastane for extra stretch. "It's built to do everything from your office meetings to hopping on a bike afterwards," says Stafford.

Todd Snyder X Sperry Top-Sider Suede Boat Shoe in Tan

Two All-American brands join forces to create a next-level boat shoe. We love the fresh updates on the classic Sperry style, including 360 degree lacing—basically one shoe lace throughout each shoe—which makes it easy to adjust your fit. And our fashion team found that its outsole is grippier than the OG model. Still, all the hallmarks of a great boat shoe are still there: a handsome suede upper that ages beautifully, and a slip-on style that'll look great for years to come.

Roger Federer x JW Anderson x Uniqlo Dry-Ex Polo Shirt

Tennis core is all the rage these days. Leave it to GOAT Roger Federer to ace the trend with his Uniqlo collection of court-side fits. Our editors love this sharp-as-hell polo, which has handsome piping along the waistband and collar, and a moisture-wicking, stink-fighting fabric to keep you feeling and looking cool all summer.

Giorgio Armani Acqua di Gio Eau de Toilette Spray

Pair your hot weather fits with a classic summer fragrance. Stafford loves this crisp, fresh scent with notes of Bergamot and cedarwood that will make you feel like you're at the Amalfi Coast, even if you're not. "It's an energizing scent both the wearer and those around him will enjoy," says Stafford.

Prince x Target Pickleball Zip-Front Warm-Up Jacket

[Best New Menswear Releases](https://weight-signal.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/8750062478.html)

The Pickleball craze continues. Tennis clothing brand Prince teamed up with Target to create a sporty new Pickleball collection, which includes this stylish warm-up jacket. We live the contrasting turtleneck and sleeves, which add a fun pop of color, and the slash pockets are roomy enough to store your pickleballs without adding much bulk. "Perfect for layering on pre- or post game, it's comfortable, warm and stylish on-and-off the court," Stafford says.
