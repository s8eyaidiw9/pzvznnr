# 8 Exercises You Can Do at Home to Build Shoulder Mass

In his latest video on YouTube, Athlean-X founder Jeff Cavaliere C.S.C.S. demonstrated a series of moves that he recommends you incorporate into your next shoulder workout. Alternating between bodyweight and dumbbells, these eight exercises can help to build strength and grow muscle mass in the deltoids.

Bodyweight Side Lateral

The relative adduction (movement back toward the body's midline) achieved in this exercise will engage the middle delt better than other moves, explains Cavaliere. "We're not just planting our arm on the ground and twisting our body in space, we're actually trying to drive the elbow into the ground," he says. "What will happen is the rest of your body will turn."

Cheat Lateral Raise to Strict Lateral Raise

[Exercises You Can Do](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/0158300026.html)

"The important thing about the lateral raise is it gives us an option to hit the middle delt again, and we know that size comes from training our middle delt," says Cavaliere. The cheat provides an opportunity to use a heavier dumbbell for eccentric overload, while the strict version can take those muscle fibers to fatigue using a lighter weight. If you're just starting out, be mindful of the weight you use for the "cheat" aspect of the movement.

Prowler Pushup

Kind of a cross between a mountain climber and a plank, his bodyweight move targets the front delt, engaging this muscle at the forward end of the move, and then achieves concentric overload at the back end of the move.

Seated Dumbbell Z Press

[at Home to Build](https://ageless-peptides.china-akan.com/)

This overhead press variation is performed sitting at a right angle, which takes away some of the additional momentum you would usually get from the legs when doing the move. "We can decrease the amount of weight here, but increase the impact the exercise has on the delts by taking away any of the assistance that might occur in a normal overhead press, and force the delts to do more work."

Frog Pushup

This pushup variation engages both the front and middle delts by utilizing a straight up-and-down motion, where the feet are only there for support. "We're almost trying to offload all of our bodyweight right over those front delts, and then work directly against gravity up and down," he says.

Dumbbell High Pull

The key to this move is the positioning of the hands in relation to the elbows, says Cavaliere. "The hands have got to be higher than the elbows," he says. "By getting the hands high and pulling, not only are we getting middle delt and rear delt activity, we're getting them with external rotation."

Handstand Pushup

"Here, we're taking virtually our entire bodyweight and having to lift it straight up and down with the help of a wall," says Cavaliere. "The beauty of this exercise is that if it's too difficult, you can always walk yourself away a little bit, creating a pike pushup position, to make it a little easier."

Dumbbell Clean and Press

Unlike the Z press, this dumbbell exercise makes the most of momentum to achieve greater overload. "We can get extension through the ankles, knees and hips to help drive the movement from below, then continue that with a push press at the top," says Cavaliere.
