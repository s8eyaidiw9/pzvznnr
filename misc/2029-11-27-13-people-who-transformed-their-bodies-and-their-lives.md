# 13 People Who Transformed Their Bodies—and Their Lives

CHANGING YOUR LIFE takes real effort. We all live with struggles and obstacles that hold us back. For some of us, there’s eventually a lightbulb moment—when you decide you’re ready to make a change.

If you’re looking for a bit of motivation to help get things going, take a look through some of our inspiring fitness transformations. A theme you might notice: Lots of these guys are dealing with health or life issues outside of their immediate control. At the end of the day, it’s not about what or how much they changed, but that they were strong enough to try at all.

We pulled together some stories of real guys that we’ve published in the past—and the humility, strength, and dedication, of their subjects shines through. Some men have weight loss stories where they lost over 200 pounds. Some guys hit their stride in run clubs or on stationary bikes. Plenty started it all by just taking a walk. And the results speak for themselves. These guys changed how we think about what’s possible. We hope they do for you, too.

Tony Varilly-Alvarado

“With my history of health problems, my cardiologist insisted I needed to lose weight. He put me in touch with a cardiometabolic specialist, who concurred. I understood then that if I didn’t take action, there was a good chance I wouldn't live a long life.

I learned to focus on how tasty the food I could eat was, instead of lamenting the lack of foods like bread, pasta, and rice in my diet. The little devil on my shoulder who whispered bad food ideas into my ear went poof!”
