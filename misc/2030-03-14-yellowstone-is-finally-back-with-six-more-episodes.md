AFTER NEARLY TWO full years off the air, Yellowstone is finally returning for the second half of its fifth season. And between remembering what, exactly, is going on in this show, Kevin Costner's departure, and whether or not season 5, part 2 really will be the end of Yellowstone or not, we've got a whole lot to talk about.

After season 5 part 1 left off with a cliffhanger—we could be in for a full-on Dutton family civil war—we've been left hanging for nearly two years. A quick recap: nothing is really all that different from the way this show always goes, but just a little more extreme. Beth Dutton (Kelly Reilly) hates Jamie Dutton. Jamie Dutton (Wes Bentley) hates Beth Dutton. That's always been the case ever since childhood trauma, but now things are elevated. Their dad, John Dutton (Kevin Costner) is now the Governor of Montana, and Jamie, who should be his top legal mind, is actually being played by a local bunch of capitalists called Market Equities. Anyway, Jamie and Beth are trying to kill each other now, and John seems very close to being swayed to Beth's side.

That's going to make season 5, part 2, particularly interesting, considering Kevin Costner's very public departure from the show. Long story short: he left to make his Horizon films, and never came back. So how will Yellowstone pick that storyline back up without John? That much remains to be seen. And it kind of makes the season even more intriguing as a result!
