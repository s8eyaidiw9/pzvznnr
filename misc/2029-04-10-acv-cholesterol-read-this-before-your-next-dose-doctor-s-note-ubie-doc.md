How does apple cider vinegar affect cholesterol levels, and what is the recommended intake?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Apple cider vinegar (ACV) may help lower cholesterol, with studies suggesting 1–2 tablespoons diluted in water daily could support reductions in total and LDL cholesterol. However, an exact recommended dosage has not been firmly established, and results vary based on individual health factors, diet, and duration of use.
