# The 'Avengers: Endgame' Cast Is Sharing Behind-the-Scenes Footage of Iron Man's Funeral

The Avengers: Endgame cast did not stick to their on set no-phone rule.

They've all been sharing behind-the-scenes footage from the movie.

Mark Ruffalo and Chris Evans recently posted photos and videos from Iron Man's funeral.

[Behind-the-Scenes Footage of](https://pepperedx.com/4u5urmhrzb/)

The cast of Avengers: Endgame were all bound to a pretty strict no-phone rule on set in order to avoid spoilers or leaks, and it seems like they all stuck to it...until a barrage of photos and videos began to hit the interwebs. From Chris Pratt's "super illegal set video" to Josh Brolin's birthday post, we know now that the cast just didn't GAF about the rules, which we fans applaud them for.

Most recently, The Hulk himself, Mark Ruffalo, posted some photos from Tony Stark's funeral—or "the wedding," as the directors called it, so the cast wouldn't know what was going on. The photos show actors including Chris Evans (Captain America), Chris Hemsworth (Thor), Don Cheadle (War Machine), Tom Holland (Spider-Man), Letitia Wright (Shuri), Danai Gurira (Okoye), Benedict Wong (Wong), Jon Favreau (Happy), and Benedict Cumberbatch (Doctor Strange) between takes.

Chris Evans decided to get into the behind-the-scenes actions as well, posting not one, but two, videos from the funeral. In the first one, he films Ruffalo as he takes photos of the funeral, and we can also see Hemsworth. The second video shows Tom Holland recording his own video, while Hemsworth jokes about the "no cameras" rule.

Oh, those Avengers.
