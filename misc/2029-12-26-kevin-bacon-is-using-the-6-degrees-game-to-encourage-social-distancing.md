# Kevin Bacon Is Using the '6 Degrees' Game to Encourage Social Distancing

Kevin Bacon is arguably the most connected man in Hollywood, to the point that a game even exists to prove exactly that. "Six Degrees of Kevin Bacon," inspired by the similar "Six Degrees of Separation," posits that any random actor can be found to have a link to Kevin Bacon through six or fewer acquaintances or co-stars.
