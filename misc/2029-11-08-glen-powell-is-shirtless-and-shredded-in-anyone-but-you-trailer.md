# Glen Powell Is Shirtless and Shredded in 'Anyone But You' Trailer

Glen Powell has been getting thirst online for a hot minute now, and his new movie Anyone But You might just cement his heartthrob status.

In the new romantic comedy, Powell plays Ben, whose first date with the beautiful Bea (Sydney Sweeney) crashes and burns, leading to a vicious animosity between the two. But as the film's teaser trailer reveals, when they are both invited to a destination wedding in Australia, they decide it will be easier to get through the celebrations if they just pretend to be a couple after all.
