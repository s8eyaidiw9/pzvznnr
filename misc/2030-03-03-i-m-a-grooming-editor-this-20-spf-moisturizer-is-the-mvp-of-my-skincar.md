Like every great sunscreen, No. 128 offers dual-spectrum protection, meaning it shields against UVA and UVB rays. Most dermatologists recommend an SPF level of at least 30 before exposure to the sun, as this will protect against 97 percent of rays. Anything less, and your skin is vulnerable to sun damage and premature signs of aging. Marlowe takes this guideline a hair further with SPF 50, offering protection against up to 98 percent of UV rays. It’s also water resistant for 80 minutes (you’ll rarely see sunscreen that lasts longer).

Of course, sun protection is only half of the SPF moisturizer equation. To match the smoothing and hydrating abilities of the best solo moisturizers, it’s also loaded with skin-boosting ingredients. First, there’s shea butter to soften skin, along with aloe vera to soothe and reduce any redness or inflammation. This is followed by a healthy dose of vitamin A, which helps repair skin cells to promote a clearer complexion and more youthful look.

But what’s not included in this formula is as useful for skin as what is. It’s entirely non-comedogenic, meaning it avoids common moisturizing oils, like coconut, avocado, or almond oil, or silicones known to clog pores and cause acne. Not to mention, there isn’t a trace of other irritants like parabens and phthalates. It’s also fragrance-free, so it won’t irritate sensitive skin or clash with your other scents, like cologne. Plus, every No. 128 tube is made from at least 50 percent recycled material.

Wear

More than any SPF product I’ve used, this one feels like a traditional moisturizer as you dispense it and apply it. It has a silk-like, milky texture that never feels heavy. It absorbs into the skin in seconds, matching the feel of A1 gel moisturizers, like Neutrogena’s Hydro Boost Water Gel (aka our best overall moisturizer). However, Hydro Boost is only concerned with, well, hydrating skin, while No. 128 matches this ability as it also protects.

The non-comedogenic formula is well executed, so the oil-free lotion never feels greasy. My skin tends to be on the drier side; however, I’d imagine (and have confirmed with other Men’s Health testers) that No. 128 is perfect for oily skin. This is in addition to feeling gentle on my dry and sensitive skin.

To be clear, No. 128 is far from a heavy cream; however, it can be easy to overapply. It can feel more lightweight than it is, especially with how quickly it absorbs, so you may feel inclined to pump more onto your face than necessary. Fight this urge! When I first tried No. 128, I overapplied, and while it still didn’t feel greasy, it did smudge and run into my eyes when I sweat or rubbed my face. But after wearing it a couple of times, it becomes clear how little is needed to fully cover and protect your face. One finger-tip-sized dab will do it.

Effectiveness

Marlowe No. 128 is designed for everyday use to hydrate and protect skin from UV rays as you commute to work, walk the dog, or perform any other daily responsibilities. It’s my go-to morning moisturizer for this reason, and also because I’m almost always running late, combining two steps in my regimen makes a big difference. I add as much walking to my commute as possible, so applying a moisturizer beforehand helps my skin look like I’ve actually slept, while it keeps it from burning is very much welcome.

Since I stopped overapplying, it’s never smudged or run into my eyes, even during the hottest of days. But daily duties are not all No. 128 can handle. While it’s not my go-to sunscreen for working out, I’ve used it in a jam or when I want to limit the amount of products I travel with, and it certainly holds up. Now, to be clear, No. 128 is not technically sweat-resistant, so I wouldn’t recommend it for a long run or hike. But for shorter workouts (like three/four-mile runs or low-impact hikes), it performs adequately.
