# Celebs Who Are Also Triathletes, From Gordon Ramsay to Jennifer Garner

[Also Triathletes](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/blog/0326535195.html)

Finding the motivation to get up and go for a run can be difficult enough—but imagine swimming and biking on top of that...while balancing the schedule of a celebrity. It turns out, many A-listers have subjected their lives (and bodies!) to just that. All of these singers, actors, and more have competed in a triathlon.

Gordon Ramsay

In 2013, the celebrity chef took his skills to the Ironman World Championship and finished at a respectable 14:04:48. Since then, he's been an active participant in Ironman competitions around the world and has taken part in countless marathons and triathlons.

Sean Astin

The Lord of the Rings star competed in the Ironman World Championship in 2015. He crossed the finish line at 15:30:31 and raised more than $25,000 for his charity #Run3rd, which provides support for running programs for underserved children, according to PEOPLE.

Max Greenfield

The New Girl actor has competed in multiple Nautica Malibu Triathlons, his first being in 2012. "It is an incredible event and it raises money for Children's Hospital Los Angeles for pediatric cancer research," Max told PEOPLE ahead of his fourth year participating in 2016. "Every dollar is necessary and goes to good use. As a parent, I can't think of anything more important."

Jennifer Lopez

J-Lo is another star who's participated in the Nautica Malibu Triathlon. She competed in the 2008 iteration, finishing the 1/2-mile swim, 18-mile bike ride, and 4-mile run in 2:23:28—and it was her first triathlon.

Matthew McConaughey

There's no doubt that Matthew McConaughey is fit, but did you know he finished the 4-mile run portion of the Nautica Malibu Triathlon in 27:25? That's just slightly under a 7 minute mile pace. His final time was 1:43:48. Alright, alright, alright.

America Ferrera

America Ferrera had a transformative experience running her first triathlon. The actress penned an op-ed about how she competed to silence the voice in her head that said she couldn't do it in The New York Times, explaining her fears and how she felt after. Oh yeah and then she completed a second triathlon...and a third.

Jeff Conine

[Who Are Also Triathletes](https://yldzdh.com/84ev3f250q/)

Jeff Conine became the first Major League baseball player to finish the Ironman World Championship in 2008. The former pro athlete earned a time of 14:43:45.

Tony Kanaan

Racecar driver Tony Kanaan has been signing up for triathlons for decades. "I started swimming when I was a little kid racing go-carts because you can't really lift weights, so I started to swim to get stronger in my upper body," Tony told Active. "And I've always biked and the run just sort of happened. I thought, 'If I'm doing all three, then I might as well motivate myself to train more,' so I signed up for my first triathlon 18 years ago and I caught the bug."

Hines Ward

The former Pittsburgh Steeler competed in the Ironman World Championship in 2013. He finished the 2.4-mile swim, 112-mile bike ride, and full marathon with a time of 13:08:15.

Mel C

Mel C lives up to her nickname Sporty Spice. The singer has been competing in triathlons around the world since 2011.

Eric Byrnes

The former MLB outfielder is a triathlon veteran, having competed in 11 full Ironman Triathlons. After his 11-year baseball career ended, he chose to pursue endurance sports. "The beautiful thing about triathlon is that it has no age limit, and the sport has nothing to do with how far or fast you go," Byrnes wrote on the Team USA site. "The only thing that really matters is that you go."

Ellie Kemper

And now we've hit The Office portion of this list. Many of the cast members have gotten together over the years to participate and relay in the Nautica Malibu Triathlon. Ellie Kemper is one of them, who lent her legs to the running portion of the event.

Rainn Wilson

Rainn Wilson, who famously portrayed Dwight Schrute in The Office, also competed alongside his costars. Too bad Jim wasn't there to pull a prank on him.

Ed Helms

Ed Helms (a.k.a. Andy from The Office) also competed with his costars during their relay version of the Nautica Malibu Triathlon.

Jimmie Johnson

NASCAR driver Jimmie Johnson has competed in about a dozen triathlons and 70.3s. He even finished one 72.3 when he made a wrong turn and added two miles to his race.

Natalie Morales

Natalie Morales started running in 11th grade, so she wasn't worried about the running leg of her race—but the swim was a different story. "I was so afraid of the swim because I swam the course the day before the race and jellyfish were everywhere," she told Triathlete. "But in the end, the swim was the most exciting because I faced my fear and pulled it off."

Don Davey

In 2012, the former NFL star completed the Ironman 70.3 Florida in 5:00:16. Since then, he's traveled to Europe to compete and went to Hawaii for the Ironman World Championships.

Jennie Finch
