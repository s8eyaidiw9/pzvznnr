# Everything We Know About Season 5 of 'Big Mouth' So Far

[About Season of Big](https://benpepsi.com/)

Inspired by co-creators Nick Kroll and Andrew Goldberg’s childhoods, Netflix's animated series, Big Mouth, is not your average coming-of-age show. It captures the awkward stage of adolescence and the unfiltered experiences that come with learning about sex and hormones.

Season 4 is now streaming, and if you’ve already binged every episode, you know the creators amped up the graphics and crude humor. Series regulars Kroll, John Mulaney, Jessi Klein, Jason Mantzoukas, Jenny Slate, Maya Rudolph, and Andrew Rannells return as their characters head off to summer camp before starting eighth grade. With the help (and oftentimes sabotage) of Maury, the hormone monster, and Connie, the hormone monstress, this season has the same hilarious take on puberty with the addition of more inclusivity, which the show has been criticized for in the past.

The storyline comes with new characters like Natalie, a transgender girl at summer camp that the characters met pre-transition, conversations about racial identity as Missy’s extended family is introduced, and topics of anxiety, fear, and depression. With each season earning over 95 percent on Rotten Tomatoes, it’s no wonder fans are already anxious for Season 5. In July 2019, Big Mouth was renewed through Season 6 even before the release of Season 3. With a new season is in the works, here’s what we might (or at least hope to) see.

What will Season 5 be about?

[We Know About Season](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/blog/0494742609.html)

Big Mouth is great when it comes to perfectly wrapping up each season but still managing to leave fans wanting more. If you made it to the Season 4 finale, you probably noticed a couple cliffhangers that hinted at possible storylines for Season 5 as the characters go through eighth grade.

Nick and Andrew's friendship is back on track and they both overcame their own mental health crises. Nick now has Rick for his hormone monster, so fans might see more romance next season since neither Nick nor Andrew had girlfriends. Jay and Lola had their epic Bonnie and Clyde relationship (which basically carried this entire season), but things got crazy when Jay dumped her in the end. Lola was furious and declared war on Jay, so this is clearly just the beginning and we’ll probably see Lola get her revenge in Season 5 some way or another.

After switching up her look and embracing her Black heritage, Missy hit it off with the newly single DeVon during the school trip to New York City. So we might see Missy pop up with a new boyfriend soon. Matthew struggled the entire season with his sexuality and by the end, finally told both of his parents he’s gay. In Season 5, fans might see him coming out of his shell more with his boyfriend Aiden now that he has the support from his dad, while still dealing with the disapproval from his mom. For Jessi, after an awful experience with anxiety and depression, her parents let her move back to Bridgeton. At the end of the season she was thrilled she could help Nick overcome his own anxiety. So, this could possibly make her revisit her feelings for him next season, since we already know Nick likes her.

During Season 4, fans also were introduced to a few new monsters, Tito the Anxiety Mosquito and the Gratitoad, and had reappearances from earlier seasons like the Shame Wizard and Depression Kitty. It’s more than likely the characters will have to deal with some new faces. Connie, the hormone monstress, even hinted at a possible Self Harm Walrus (yikes) during Season 4, when she warned Jessi to call her if he ever showed up. Looks like more supernatural beings are coming, especially with the introduction of the upcoming Big Mouth spinoff, Human Resources: We Manage People, which will focus on the world of the monsters. No, the Season 5 storyline hasn’t been announced but there are quite a few holes in the plot for fans to linger on in the meantime.

Who will be cast in Season 5?

The main voice cast is set to return for season 5, with the storyline heavily following Nick (Nick Kroll), Andrew (John Mulaney), and Jessi (Jessi Klein). Earlier this year, in the midst of the Black Lives Matter movement throughout the country, Jenny Slate announced that she would step down from her role as Missy, expressing the need to allow Black actors and actresses to portray Black characters. Slate continued the role throughout the majority of Season 4 until actress and comedian Ayo Edebiri took over for the last two episodes. So, we can expect to see Edebiri really come into her new role voicing Missy for Season 5. The timing seems almost perfect as Missy’s character is starting to embrace her racial identity in the show.

As usual, Big Mouth also has the most hilarious guest stars. Season 4 gave us appearances from Seth Rogan, Quinta Brunson, Lena Waithe, John Oliver, Josie Totah, Paul Giamatti, Sterling K. Brown, Maya Erskine, and Anna Konkle. There’s sure to be more additions next season.

When will Season 5 be released?

[of Big](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/blog/3004588526.html)

Netflix has tried to stay consistent by releasing one season of Big Mouth each year, usually around late September and early October. With the coronavirus pandemic pushing the Season 4 release back to early December 2020, it’s likely that Season 5 will be released late 2021, or even early 2022 if delays continue. But hope isn’t lost yet because Nick Kroll, co-creator and star, hinted at Season 5 already being in the works back in March 2020 during a Big Mouth table read.
