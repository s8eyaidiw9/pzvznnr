How can you tell if your partner might have sleep apnea?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Loud snoring paired with daytime fatigue is a classic warning sign of sleep apnea — a common but serious condition where breathing repeatedly stops during sleep. Other clues include gasping, choking, or long pauses in breathing at night, morning headaches, and trouble concentrating during the day.

Left untreated, sleep apnea can raise the risk of high blood pressure, heart disease, stroke, and diabetes. The good news: it's highly treatable once diagnosed.

Sleep apnea is a common condition where a person has trouble breathing while sleeping. This can cause loud snoring and make them very tired during the day. If your partner snores a lot, it might affect your sleep too, making you feel tired and stressed.

Signs Your Partner Might Have Sleep Apnea:

Loud Snoring: Snoring is a key sign. It might be so loud that it wakes you up.

Pauses in Breathing: You might notice that your partner stops breathing for a few seconds while sleeping.
