Though in today’s post-pandemic world, comfort continues to rule supreme, let's still be clear: we would never recommend choosing substance over style. Stylish leather sneakers generally fall into one of three categories. First, we have authentic sportswear or lifestyle brands that have solidified classic, timeless sneaker silhouettes—we're talking Nike-checked Air Force 1's and Blazers, Reebok Club C 85 Vintages, and Chuck Taylor All-Stars. Second is storied shoemakers and specialized brands that pride themselves on beautiful design and craftsmanship; for example, ever since Common Projects first introduced the idea of a premium, minimalist silhouette in 2004, the world hasn't looked back since. (Brands like Greats and Koio were quick to follow, so now it seems that every lifestyle brand has its own take on the simple lace-up trainer.)

That brings us to the third category—fashion sneakers. Perhaps athletic or niche brands aren’t your thing and you’d prefer a more luxurious take on the trend. We all know someone who skews more Allen Edmonds than Stan Smith when it comes to contrast heel tabs, or who prefers Gucci stripes to Adidas ones. No matter your preference, rest assured that there’s a place for everyone in the sweep of leather sneakers—our 20 best leather sneakers for men below prove that.
