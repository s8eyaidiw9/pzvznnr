1. Why Does Heartburn Happen on Wegovy?

Wegovy is a glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist. Its main actions are:

Slowing gastric emptying (your stomach stays fuller longer)

Suppressing appetite in the brain

Enhancing insulin secretion when blood sugar is high

Slower gastric emptying can lead to:

Increased stomach pressure

Acid backing up into the esophagus (heartburn)

A sensation of fullness, bloating, or indigestion

Most clinical trials report gastrointestinal side effects in roughly 20–30% of participants, usually early in treatment and often mild.

2. Typical vs. Concerning Heartburn

Typical (Mild to Moderate)

These are common, expected, and often improve over time as your body adjusts:

Occasional burning sensation in the chest after meals

Mild acid taste in the mouth

Heartburn that responds to lifestyle tweaks or over-the-counter (OTC) antacids

Concerning (Red Flags)

Heartburn may signal something more serious if you experience:

Severe chest pain that radiates to jaw, neck, back or arms

[Urgent Red Flags to](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/)

Difficulty swallowing (feeling food is stuck in your throat)

Unintended weight loss, despite appetite changes

Vomiting blood or black, tarry stools

Persistent nausea/vomiting that prevents fluid intake

If you have any of these, speak to a doctor right away—they could indicate esophageal injury, ulcers, or even heart issues.

If your symptoms are mild and match the "Common Adjustment Period," try these strategies:

Lifestyle & Dietary Tweaks

Eat smaller, more frequent meals.

Avoid trigger foods: spicy dishes, coffee, citrus, tomato products, chocolate, mint, and fatty/fried foods.

Stay upright for at least 30 minutes after eating.

Don't lie down or bend over immediately after meals.

Wear loose-fitting clothes around the waist.

H2 blockers (e.g., famotidine) to reduce acid production.

Proton pump inhibitors (PPIs) (e.g., omeprazole) for more persistent symptoms—use under guidance.

Additional Tips

Chew sugar-free gum or suck on lozenges to boost saliva, which can help clear acid.

Elevate the head of your bed by 6–8 inches if you experience nighttime reflux.

5. When Heartburn Persists Beyond 4 Weeks

Persistent heartburn beyond 4 weeks on a stable Wegovy dose warrants:

A review of your current medications (including OTC and supplements)

An evaluation for gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD)

Potential dosing adjustments or timing changes with Wegovy—sometimes taking it at a different time of day helps

Always loop in your prescribing physician or a gastroenterologist before making major changes.

6. Rare but Serious Complications

Though uncommon, GLP-1 receptor agonists can be linked to:

Pancreatitis (inflammation of the pancreas), which may present as severe upper abdominal pain radiating to the back, nausea, and vomiting

Gallbladder issues—rapid weight loss can increase risk of gallstones, leading to right-upper-quadrant pain

Esophagitis or esophageal ulcers if acid exposure is severe and untreated

Symptoms like constant, severe abdominal pain or any sign of internal bleeding should prompt immediate medical attention.

7. Talking to Your Doctor

Be ready to discuss:

The onset, frequency, and severity of your heartburn

Any red-flag symptoms (e.g., blood in vomit, difficulty swallowing)

A complete list of all your medications and supplements

Your diet and lifestyle habits that might influence reflux

Your provider may recommend:

Adjusting your Wegovy dose or injection timing

Prescribing a stronger acid-blocking medication

Referring you for an endoscopy to inspect the esophagus and stomach

8. Preventing Future Episodes

Once you and your doctor have ironed out the best routine:

Stick to a heartburn-friendly diet and meal schedule.

Build in regular check-ins to assess how you're tolerating Wegovy.

Keep lifestyle factors in check—stress, sleep, and exercise all affect digestion.

9. When in Doubt, Take Action

If you notice any severe or unusual symptoms—or if your heartburn is not improving with the above steps—don't wait.
