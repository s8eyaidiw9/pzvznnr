# Everything We Know About Season 3 of FX's 'Mayans M.C.'

If you're looking for a show to binge that's edgier and way more exciting than your typical Netflix find, you're in luck—the criminally underrated Mayans M.C. on FX is Narcos meets Breaking Bad. A spinoff of the mega-successful Sons of Anarchy, the show follows a young college-bound kid, EZ Reyes, who, by no fault of his own, gets caught up in cartel violence along the U.S.-Mexico border and wants revenge.

Two seasons later, the show has become a critical hit and fan favorite. As for news of a Season 3, the show was renewed ahead of the Season 2 finale, meaning you can expect plenty more from the series soon. Here's everything we know about Season 3 of Mayans M.C., including a release date, trailer, spoilers, and more.

When is Mayans M.C. Season 3 airing?

It's been over a year since Season 2 aired, but the show is coming back with a vengeance on March 16 with not one, but two new episodes.

Mayans showrunner Elgin James had this to say about the new season: "Season 3 picks up just a few months after the slaughter of the Vatos Malditos," he told Entertainment Weekly. "EZ, now fully patched, struggles to find his footing within the club's hierarchy and, haunted by his murder of Dita, finds himself torn between darkness and the gravitational pull of his new love interest. Angel, gutted after having Adelita and his child ripped away from him, loses himself in sex, booze, and violence, until a shot at redemption lands on his doorstep."
