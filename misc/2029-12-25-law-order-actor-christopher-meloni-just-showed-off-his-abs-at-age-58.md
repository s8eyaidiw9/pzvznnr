# 'Law & Order' Actor Christopher Meloni Just Showed Off His Abs at Age 58

Christopher Meloni's imposing frame lends an intense, looming physicality to his television roles, whether he's playing the sociopathic inmate Chris Keller in HBO's gritty prison drama Oz, or upstanding hero Detective Eliot Stabler in the Dick Wolf procedural Law & Order: Special Victims Unit.

The actor's latest role is the same one we're all playing: that of a person who is self-isolating in order to minimize the risk of spreading coronavirus. And like all of us, he's getting bored (and perhaps a little frisky) from being cooped up, and is passing the time by showing off his muscular physique on Twitter.
