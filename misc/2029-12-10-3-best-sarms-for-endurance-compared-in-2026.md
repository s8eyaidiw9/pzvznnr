Types of Endurance

Endurance can be divided into two aspects; cardiovascular and muscular.

Total fitness requires that a person have high levels of both!

Cardiovascular Endurance

Cardio endurance involves aerobic metabolism – it is your ability to perform exercises that tax your heart and lungs for a prolonged period of time.

Cardiovascular endurance exercises include running, cycling, and rowing.

In fact, any activity that challenges your heart and lungs to work harder can be considered cardiovascular and this could include some forms of weight training.

Muscular Endurance

Muscular endurance specifically refers to the ability of an individual muscle to perform a high number of repetitions of a movement that takes it through its full range of motion.

This obviously relates to performing resistance exercises in the gym.

However, it can also be achieved with cardiovascular exercise.

For example, if you run for an hour, you are performing repetitions of moving your quadriceps up and down. This is a test of muscular endurance.

At the same time, you are testing the cardiovascular endurance of your heart and lungs.
