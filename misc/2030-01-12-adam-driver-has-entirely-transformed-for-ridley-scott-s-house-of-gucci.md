But it wasn't just any photo of Lady Gaga and Adam Driver—it was a photo of Lady Gaga and Adam Driver on the set of their new Ridley Scott-directed movie House of Gucci, and the transformations are remarkable. "Signore e Signora Gucci #HouseOfGucci," she wrote, and that was all it took to set the internet on fire.

As Gaga's caption alludes, she and Driver are playing husband and wife Patrizia Reggiani and Maurizio Gucci in Ridley Scott's true crime drama House of Gucci, due out in November. We can talk about a lot of things in our free time, but it's always fun to have a bit of a distraction. Both transformations here are remarkable, but lets focus in on Mr. Driver for the time being.

With House of Gucci being a Ridley Scott true crime drama that not only stars Gaga but also Al Pacino, Jared Leto, and Jeremy Irons, well, it seems like it won't be quite as big as Star Wars, but will definitely be an event movie.
