Shooting on Martin Scorsese's next movie might have been postponed due to the ongoing coronavirus pandemic, but two of its two stars, Robert De Niro and Leonardo DiCaprio, are using that delay to raise money for a good cause — and offer one lucky fan the chance to appear in the film with them.

The two actors just filmed a video calling for donations to America's Food Fund, the organization DiCaprio helped create, as part of the All In Challenge."Our most vulnerable communities need our support now more than ever," De Niro said, explaining that fans who make a donation to America's Food Fund will be in with the chance of a walk-on role in the movie.

"Bob and I are going to be starring in a film called Killers of the Flower Moon," DiCaprio said. "If you've ever wondered what it was like to work with the great Martin Scorsese, this is your chance."

The winner of the challenge will be cast as an extra in Killers of the Flower Moon. They'll spend the day on the set of the movie with DiCaprio, De Niro and Scorsese, and will be invited to attend the premiere. All you have to do is visit AllInChallenge.com and donate whatever you can.

Killers of the Flower Moon is based on the true-crime book of the same name by David Grann, and details an investigation by the newly founded FBI into a series of murders in Osage County, Oklahoma in the 1920s. It will mark De Niro and DiCaprio's first feature-length collaboration since 1993's This Boy's Life (they also worked together on Scorsese's short film The Audition in 2015).
