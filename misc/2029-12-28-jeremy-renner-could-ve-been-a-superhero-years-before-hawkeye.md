The upcoming Hawkeye series on Disney+ serves as an interesting time-marker. It marks a decade in the Marvel Cinematic Universe for star Jeremy Renner, who first debuted his take on the character in an uncredited appearance in 2011's Thor and has since played him in four more feature films (and provided his voice in a couple episodes of What If...?).

[Before Hawkeye](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/1092503059.html)

Hawkeye, though, does mark Renner's first time in the leading role, coming just about half a year after his fellow longtime Marvel star Scarlett Johansson finally got her own starring vehicle with the Black Widow film. But, as it turns out, Renner had a chance way earlier to lead a superhero franchise—in Gulliermo del Toro's 2004 Hellboy movie—but he turned it down.

“I said no to more money than I’ll probably ever make,” Renner, who also stars in the new Paramount+ series Mayor of Kingstown, said in a new Men's Health cover story. “I was focused on what I wanted to do and how. I just felt if I wasn’t connected to the material—even though it was a great director and a great job—I don’t know how I’m going to do anything exceptional.”
