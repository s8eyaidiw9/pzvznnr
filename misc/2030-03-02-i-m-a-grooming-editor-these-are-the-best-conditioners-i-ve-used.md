In fact, conditioners are especially crucial for men. "Most men shampoo their hair daily, or more frequently than women, causing their hair to become dry," says celebrity hairstylist Ryan Richman. "Shampooing cleanses your hair, but it can also strip it of natural oils that keep your hair healthy and shiny. Using conditioner will help smooth the cuticle and replace moisture."

As a general rule, you should really be using a conditioner every time you shampoo to avoid your hair getting dry or frizzy. Besides just keeping your scalp moisturized, the best conditioners for men can also pump up the volume and give your hair a thicker appearance. So if hair loss or thinning hair is a concern for you, consider one a necessity (and look for lightweight versions that say volumizing or thickening on the label).

But even if your hair is low-key thick and you've never struggled with dryness, a stellar conditioner is still crucial in not only continuing to avoid these developments, but also maintaining the health of your hair overall. Of course, everybody's hair is different so the best conditioners for men should be chosen based on your specific hair type and what it needs.

What to Consider

Ingredients

When choosing a conditioner, look for nourishing ingredients that match your unique hair type and its subsequent needs. Ingredients like shea butter and coconut oil hydrate and smooth, while Argan oil and jojoba oil repair and add shine. For strengthening, keratin and panthenol (aka vitamin B5) work wonders, while tea tree oil and rosemary oil promote scalp health and hair growth. Aloe vera and avocado oil provide moisture and softness, and glycerin helps retain hydration. If you prefer natural shine without buildup, opt for silicone-free options like Moringa or sunflower seed oil.

Hair Type

All conditioners for men should contain some sort of moisturizing and hydrating ingredients. These are often found in the form of plant oils, glycerin, hyaluronic acid and others. But that’s just a baseline. To really know what to look for in a conditioner, you need to consider your hair type. Different hair types require different amounts of moisture, so by extension, they require different types of conditioners. The following are some common hair types to keep in mind and what to look for in a conditioner.

Straight

Straight hair can be either dry or oily, but tends to lack natural volume or fullness. Look for conditioners that are lightweight (so they won’t weigh down straight hair) and are also volumizing or thickening.

Curly

Curly hair tends to need more moisturization because the natural oils from your scalp have a harder time reaching the ends of the hair, leaving it prone to dryness and frizziness. Look for conditioners that contain plant oils (jojoba and Argan are good bets) but are still lightweight enough that they won’t weigh down curls.

Textured

Textured hair needs the most moisture, so heavier conditioners that contain ingredients like shea butter and cocoa butter will not only help moisturize but also seal that moisture in better. You may want to invest in a leave-in conditioner for further moisturizing power, as well. A good rule of thumb is to look for conditioners specifically formulated for textured hair.

Oily

It may seem counterintuitive to apply more moisture to oily hair, but moisture and oil are completely different animals. If you have oily hair, you may be able to get away with conditioning your hair less often, but when you do (again, you should every time you shampoo), look for oily-hair formulas, many of which contain oil-absorbing ingredients.

Thinning

The biggest thing to consider when using a conditioner on thinning hair is the weight. Opt for lightweight conditioners that won’t weigh down thin or fine hair. Some conditioners formulated for thinning hair may also contain ingredients like biotin, keratin, and others that help to strengthen and plump hair to make it appear thicker.

Damaged

If you dye your hair or use hot tools frequently, you’ll want to use a conditioner that helps build hair back up to a healthy state. Look for bottles that say things like strengthening or bond building.

Size

When it comes to choosing the perfect conditioner, the first step is all about finding one that complements your hair’s unique needs and your personal preferences. But once you’ve discovered that ideal conditioner that works wonders on your locks, it's important to think about the size that best fits your routine.

If you’re always on the go or travel frequently, smaller bottles (think 3.4 oz or less for airport-friendly convenience) are your best bet. These compact sizes fit neatly into your Dopp kit and are perfect for stashing in your carry-on. On the other hand, if your conditioner stays mostly at home and you’re using it daily, it makes more sense to go for a larger bottle. Not only will you save money in the long run, but you’ll have more product to keep your hair looking its best.
