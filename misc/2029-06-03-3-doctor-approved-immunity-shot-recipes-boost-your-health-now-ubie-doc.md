1. Citrus-Ginger Vitamin C Shot

Vitamin C is essential for immune cells to function optimally and for skin barrier defense. Carr & Maggini (2017) report that regular intake supports both innate and adaptive immunity. This bright, tangy shot packs over 150 mg of vitamin C per serving.

Ingredients (makes 2 shots)

2 medium oranges (approx. 200 ml fresh juice)

1 large lemon (juice)

1 small grapefruit (juice)

2 cm fresh ginger root, peeled

1 tablespoon raw honey (optional, for taste)

Key Benefits

High-dose vitamin C to support white blood cell activity

Ginger's anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial compounds

Natural antioxidants to neutralize free radicals

Instructions

Peel and chop ginger; place in a blender with a splash of orange juice.

Blend until smooth.

Juice the remaining oranges, grapefruit and lemon.

Strain citrus juice into a pitcher to remove pulp if you prefer a smoother shot.

Stir in the ginger "puree" and honey until well mixed.

Divide into two shot glasses and serve immediately.

Tip: For a spicier kick, leave some ginger fibers in your shot.

2. Turmeric-Spinach Zinc Booster Shot

Zinc plays a gatekeeper role in immunity, regulating cell development and inflammatory response (Wessels et al., 2017). Spinach and pumpkin seeds are plant-based zinc sources; turmeric and black pepper enhance anti-inflammatory action.

1 cup fresh spinach leaves

2 tablespoons raw pumpkin seeds (shelled)

1 cm fresh turmeric root (or ½ tsp turmeric powder)

1 cm fresh ginger root

½ green apple, cored

Pinch of black pepper (to boost turmeric absorption)

100 ml water

Zinc for immune cell maturation and function

Curcumin (turmeric) to modulate inflammation

Antioxidant support from greens

If using turmeric and ginger roots, peel and roughly chop.

In a small blender, combine spinach, pumpkin seeds, turmeric, ginger, apple and water.

Blend at high speed until smooth.

Strain through a fine mesh strainer or nut milk bag for a smoother shot.

Stir in the black pepper, pour into two small glasses, and enjoy immediately.

Tip: Soak pumpkin seeds in water for 2 hours beforehand to improve nutrient absorption and blendability.

Tips for Maximum Impact

Prepare shots fresh daily; nutrients like vitamin C degrade quickly once exposed to air.

Drink on an empty stomach or between meals for better absorption.

Store any leftover shots in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 24 hours.

Pair with a balanced diet rich in lean proteins, healthy fats and whole grains for sustained immune support.
