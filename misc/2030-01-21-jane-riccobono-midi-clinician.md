[Riccobono Midi Clinician](https://barrierwatch.bbsxyxy.com/)

Jane Riccobono, MSN, APRN, WHNP-BC

Drawn to perimenopause care & beyond: “I have been practicing as a Women’s Health Nurse Practitioner and Certified Nurse Midwife for seven years. I was initially drawn to this field because I wanted to serve underserved communities, particularly in midwifery and perimenopause care. Guided by my own experiences as a patient, my care approach is rooted in listening, collaborating, and providing compassionate care.”

Changing the system for women: “Too many women are failed by the healthcare system as it is. I chose to work for Midi because I wanted to be part of changing that. I love helping women feel seen, heard, and understood, especially when it comes to the complex challenges of midlife. Every patient deserves to know their options, have their concerns taken seriously, and feel confident in the next steps for their health.”

Seeing real results: “The most rewarding part of my job is hearing from patients about how much better they feel. Knowing that my care has made a positive impact on someone’s life—whether it’s reducing symptoms, improving well-being, or simply feeling heard—is why I do this work.”
