Peripheral Neuropathy: Foot Symptoms Neurologists Use to Narrow Down the Cause

Peripheral neuropathy occurs when the nerves that carry signals between your brain and spinal cord and the rest of your body are damaged. In many cases, the feet are the first place people notice changes. Understanding the specific neuropathy symptoms in your feet can help a neurologist pinpoint the cause and guide you toward the right treatment.

When to Seek Immediate Medical Attention

Many neuropathies progress slowly, but some situations require urgent care:

Sudden or rapidly worsening weakness

Loss of bowel or bladder control

Severe burns or injuries you do not feel on your feet

Signs of infection around ulcers or wounds

If you experience any of these, speak to a doctor or go to the emergency department right away.
