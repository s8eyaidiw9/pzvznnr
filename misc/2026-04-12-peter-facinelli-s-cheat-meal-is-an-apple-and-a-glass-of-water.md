# Peter Facinelli's 'Cheat Meal' Is an Apple and a Glass of Water

Actor Peter Facinelli, who you might know best from the classic 1998 teen rom-com Can't Hardly Wait or the tween-targeted mega franchise that was Twilight, knows a thing or two about staying in shape after more than two decades in Hollywood. (Some might argue, the 46-year-old is a vampire, based on his always-in-shape physique alone.)

[Peter Facinelli's Cheat](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/6450761795.html)

For the latest installment of Gym & Fridge, the actor gave Men's Health an inside look at his Los Angeles home, including his make-shift outdoor gym and the interior of his fridge, and shared the many ways he keeps in A-list shape.

For Facinelli, keeping fit begins with getting some good ole-fashioned fresh-air. "I sometimes bike ride for cardio," he said. "I roller blade for cardio. Being outdoors is awesome. It takes your mind off of things."

As a guy who sees workouts as more of a chore than a hobby, he's also all about switching his routine up as much as possible for his own sanity; he recommends others do the same. "Force yourself to do something every day," he said, "Even if it's like 15 push-ups, 20 push-ups, just do it it because you get into a habit of skipping workouts and just don't do it."

As for his diet, well, Facinelli's a simple guy. Coconut water, turkey burgers, and other lean proteins line his fridge's shelves. His optimal protein shake, however, is a little more complicated: peanut butter, a banana, almond milk, and two scoops of protein powder, with CBD added. "It's better than ice cream," he said. (Ironically, ice cream is the actor's weakness. "If you're like me and you like ice cream, don't buy it because then you're not tempted.")

Staying in shape while traveling for work's tough, but the actor has a secret weapon when it comes to resisting temptations: meal planning. "If I don't, and I'm hungry, and all of a sudden I'll be like, 'Oh a bag of M&M's,'" he said. "Whatever's in front of me I gotta eat. I made a conscious choice now toward what's going in my body."

Of course, he does have his 2am indulgences—so he says. "If I'm starving, I'll come downstairs and have like half an apple with some peanut butter and a glass of water," he said. "You don't want to eat too much in the middle of the night." We tend to fall more into the eat-whatever's-readily-available-in-our-fridge, but to each their own.
