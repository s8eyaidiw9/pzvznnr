American Olympic hurdler and bobsledder Lolo Jones has been open about her virginity in the past, but on the latest episode of Kevin Hart’s “Laugh Out Loud” series Cold as Balls, she admits “that was a mistake.”

While sitting in a tub of freezing ice along side Hart, Jones, 37, said, “That killed all my dates after that. Like didn’t even have a chance. Before, at least I had a chance.”
