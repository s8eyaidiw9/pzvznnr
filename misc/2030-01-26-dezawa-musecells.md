THE SCIENCE

What Are Dezawa MuseCells®?

MUSE stands for Multilineage-differentiating Stress-Enduring — a rare subpopulation of pluripotent-like stem cells discovered by Professor Mari Dezawa at Tohoku University in Japan in 2010. Unlike conventional mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs), Muse cells are biologically distinct. They exist naturally within your own tissues — in bone marrow, connective tissue, and peripheral blood — yet make up only 1–2% of a standard MSC sample. This rarity is precisely what makes them so clinically significant.

Muse cells are stress-resilient by nature. Where other stem cells struggle to survive in inflamed or damaged tissue, Muse cells persist, migrate to areas of injury, and differentiate into the precise cell types needed for repair — without genetic reprogramming, and without immune suppression.

WHAT MAKES THEM DIFFERENT

Six Properties That Define Muse Cells

When administered intravenously, Muse cells circulate and migrate toward signals of injury and inflammation. They do not need to be directed to a specific site — they detect damage and begin the repair process autonomously.

Muse cells can differentiate in vivo into cells from all three embryonic germ layers — mesoderm, ectoderm, and endoderm. This gives them exceptional versatility across orthopedic, neurological, cardiac, and aesthetic applications.

Unlike embryonic stem cells or iPSCs, Muse cells carry no meaningful risk of abnormal cell growth. Their safety profile across multiple clinical studies is a defining advantage over other pluripotent-capable therapies.

Most stem cells lose potency in inflamed or hypoxic environments. MUSE cells thrive under precisely those conditions — the environments where regenerative activity is needed most. Their resilience is intrinsic, not engineered.

MUSE cells exhibit low immunogenicity — meaning donor cells can be received without HLA matching or immunosuppression. Allogeneic administration is safe and practical for nearly all eligible patients.

Dezawa MuseCells® used at Extension Health are perinatal in origin, derived from donated umbilical cord tissue — a non-invasive, ethical source that does not involve embryonic material of any kind.
