Gabrielle Kimble, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

Experience rooted in compassion and growth: "I’ve been working in healthcare since 2008, and over the years, I’ve worked in several cardiology departments, the emergency room, and the ICU. I’ve also spent time in aesthetics and urgent care, where I discovered how much I enjoy connecting with patients and helping them feel their best inside and out."

Filling the gaps in women’s care: "Throughout my years in medicine, I began to see how often women’s symptoms were brushed off or treated with quick fixes that didn’t address what was really going on. That realization, along with my growing interest in functional medicine, led me to Midi. I was drawn to how Midi uses an integrative approach and truly listens to women, helping them feel seen, understood, and well again — not just managed."

Listening first, healing together: "For me, care starts with listening. My goal is to make every woman feel heard, supported, and understood. Together, we’ll create a plan that focuses on your overall well-being, supporting your body and mind with honesty and care every step of the way."

Finding balance: "Outside of work, I’m married with a daughter and one very loved dog. I find balance through hot yoga and traveling whenever I can."
