Lymph nodes in the neck and jaw are located in several distinct groups. The main clusters include the submandibular nodes (beneath the jawbone), submental nodes (under the chin), cervical nodes (along the sides of the neck), and supraclavicular nodes (just above the collarbone). These nodes play a key role in filtering harmful substances and supporting the immune system.

If you've noticed swelling, tenderness, or lumps in these areas, it's important not to guess. Swollen lymph nodes can signal anything from a minor infection to something requiring medical attention — and identifying the cause early makes a real difference. Explanation

Lymph nodes in the neck and jaw area are part of the body's immune system and help fight infections. These nodes are grouped in specific areas:

Submandibular Lymph Nodes: These are located beneath the jawbone and are responsible for draining lymphatic fluid from the mouth, lips, and cheeks.
