# Here's Why Chrissy Teigen No Longer Drinks Alcohol

Attendees of the inaugural Chase Sapphire Sundays: Brunch with Chrissy Teigen event held on August 20 were seen sipping on unlimited glasses of rosé and other specialty cocktails the entire time. However, Teigen wasn’t among those imbibing at Rose Café in Venice, California.

Teigen told Cosmopolitan that she recently did a “reset on her health,” which includes cutting out alcohol.

"I was, point blank, just drinking too much," she told the magazine. "I got used to being in hair and makeup and having a glass of wine. Then that glass of wine would carry over into me having one before the awards show. And then a bunch at the awards show. And then I felt bad for making kind of an ass of myself to people that I really respected. And that feeling, there's just nothing like that. You feel horrible. It's not a good look for me, for John, for anybody."

Teigen, who has been the spokesperson for Captain Morgan and Smirnoff, went on to say that she has a history of alcohol abuse in her family, and that she’s not the type of person who can only have one drink and call it quits.

"Imagine everything we have," Teigen said. "Everything is there, always. I don't know how to go to an awards show and not drink."

Teigen said she and husband John Legend want to start trying to have another child soon, which means IVF treatments on top of the medications she is already taking for postpartum depression and anxiety. Neither of these things goes well with alcohol, she said, so she knew she had to stop drinking.

“People think it's cutesy and fun to go on these boozy brunches, but there's more to it. I've never once been like, 'I'm sure glad I had that boozy brunch!'"
