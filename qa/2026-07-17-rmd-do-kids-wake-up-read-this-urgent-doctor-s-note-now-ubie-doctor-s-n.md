Do children with Sleep-Related Rhythmic Movement Disorder (RMD) wake up during episodes, or remain asleep?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Sleep-Related Rhythmic Movement Disorder (RMD) in children involves repetitive movements such as head banging, body rocking, or head rolling that occur as the child falls asleep or during sleep. Most children remain asleep throughout these episodes and have no memory of them the next day. While RMD is often harmless and outgrown, it can sometimes disrupt sleep quality or, in rare cases, cause injury.
