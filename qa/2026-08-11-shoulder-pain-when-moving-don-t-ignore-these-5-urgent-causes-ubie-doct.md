Explanation

Shoulder pain during movement can happen for several reasons. One common cause is problems with the rotator cuff, which is a group of muscles and tendons that help lift and rotate your arm. These issues can be due to wear and tear or injury.

Another reason for shoulder pain can be related to diabetes. People with diabetes may experience shoulder problems more often, which can include stiffness and pain.

Here are some possible causes of shoulder pain:

Rotator Cuff Tendinopathy: This is when the tendons in the shoulder become irritated or damaged, leading to pain, especially when moving the arm.

Diabetes-Related Issues: Diabetes can lead to conditions like frozen shoulder, where the shoulder becomes stiff and painful.

Other Injuries: Injuries from falls or overuse can also cause shoulder pain.
