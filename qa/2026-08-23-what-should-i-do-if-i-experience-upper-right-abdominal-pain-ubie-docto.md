Explanation

If you experience pain in the upper right part of your abdomen, first monitor your symptoms carefully. Pay attention to pain triggers and watch for additional symptoms like fever, nausea, vomiting, or changes in stool or urine appearance. These accompanying symptoms may indicate a more serious condition, such as a gallbladder or liver problem. Contact a healthcare provider if your pain is constant, worsening, or interfering with daily activities. Your doctor will likely review your medical history, perform an examination, and may order tests such as an ultrasound—a safe imaging method to examine your gallbladder and surrounding organs.
