Explanation

Pelvic pressure and bloating can be uncomfortable and may have several causes. Ovarian disorders are one possible cause. These can include conditions like ovarian cysts or other abnormalities that might be detected in primary care settings. Such conditions can lead to feelings of fullness or pressure in the pelvic area.

Another common cause is uterine fibroids. Fibroids are non-cancerous growths in the uterus that can vary in size. They can cause symptoms like pelvic pressure, bloating, and sometimes pain. According to the medical literature, fibroids are a frequent cause of these symptoms in women and can be managed in various ways, including medical and surgical treatments.

If you experience persistent pelvic pressure and bloating, it is important to consult with a healthcare provider to determine the exact cause and discuss appropriate management options.
