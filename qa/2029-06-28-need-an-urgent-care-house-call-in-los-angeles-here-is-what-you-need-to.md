Emergency Care Vs. House Calls

There are many instances where you should seek emergency care. These include (but are not limited to) fainting, speech changes, numbness on one side, concussion or damage to the head, serious burns, seizures, cuts or lacerations that may need stitches, or broken bones. Many of these symptoms can be attributed to serious conditions that require immediate care or even calling 911.

House calls are a great option when you don’t feel well and don’t want to leave your home or don’t want to expose community members to your illness. During COVID, this is a particularly helpful choice. This is a more appropriate option if you feel you have an infection, cold, flu, allergies, or other issues that are non-life-threatening.
