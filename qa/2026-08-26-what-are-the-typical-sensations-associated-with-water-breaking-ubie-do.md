Many women say that when their water breaks, they feel a sudden release of fluid from their vagina. This fluid is usually clear and water-like, and it may feel warm—almost like a tiny shower. Sometimes, it comes as a quick burst or even a steady leak. Unlike normal vaginal discharge that tends to be thicker or milky, and builds up slowly, the feeling of water breaking is more immediate and noticeable. Some women feel a rush of fluid all at once, while others might notice a small trickle that gets heavier. It is not typically painful itself, though it might be surprising because it signals that the amniotic sac has ruptured. This sensation is different from other pregnancy signs like contractions, which feel like squeezing or tightening. If you ever think your water is breaking, it’s important to tell your healthcare provider so they can check and make sure everything is okay with you and your baby.
