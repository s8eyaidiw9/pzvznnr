Can certain medications trigger sleep myoclonus?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

[Common Meds Triggering Sleep](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

Internal Medicine

Yes, certain medications can trigger sleep myoclonus. Common culprits include drugs used to treat Parkinson's disease (such as levodopa), antidepressants (especially SSRIs and tricyclics), opioids, and some antipsychotics. These medications can affect neurotransmitter activity in the brain, leading to involuntary muscle jerks during sleep.

If you're experiencing sudden muscle twitches at night and suspect your medication may be the cause, it's important not to guess or stop your prescription on your own.
