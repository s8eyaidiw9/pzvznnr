How long does it take for ibuprofen to start working?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Ibuprofen typically begins working within 30 minutes to 1 hour after being taken by mouth. Peak pain and inflammation relief usually occurs around 1 to 2 hours after the dose, and effects generally last 4 to 6 hours. Onset can vary based on factors like whether it's taken with food, the dosage, and individual metabolism. Taking ibuprofen on an empty stomach may lead to faster relief but can increase the risk of stomach irritation.

If your pain, fever, or inflammation isn't improving as expected—or you're unsure whether ibuprofen is the right choice for your symptoms—it's worth digging deeper.
