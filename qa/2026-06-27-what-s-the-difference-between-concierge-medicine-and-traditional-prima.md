What is the difference between concierge medicine and primary care?

Traditional primary care physicians (PCPs) accept payment from health insurance providers. Concierge medicine providers only accept payment from patients directly, either charging per-service or offering monthly memberships with a set range of services.

By not accepting insurance, concierge providers are able to avoid the heavy administrative burdens of filing insurance claims, allowing them to run practices with a stronger emphasis on patient care. Concierge providers typically have fewer patients, allowing them to offer longer appointments and greater appointment availability.

Concierge providers are required to have the same licenses and training as PCPs, so you can feel confident you are getting the same level of care. Both typically practice general medicine, and often serve as the first point of contact when patients have health issues to address.

You may be wondering if concierge medicine is worth the investment. While you may be satisfied with your current PCP, there are benefits to concierge medicine worth consideration, including:

House call services: Many concierge providers offer services in the comfort of your home or office, sending health experts to you instead of making you wait in reception. For those with chaotic schedules, concierge medicine can help take the stress out of getting the healthcare you need.

Stronger doctor-patient relationships: In addition to longer appointments, many concierge providers also offer telehealth services. Being able to get questions answered as they come up can mean catching health issues early, leading to better overall outcomes.

Family treatment: Having to schedule multiple appointments with different healthcare providers to get the whole family treated can be challenging. Mobile concierge providers will often allow you to schedule multiple services for the same visit, meaning you can get the entire family treated at the same time.

Avoid unnecessary exposure to Covid-19 and its variants: If you or a loved one is at high risk of hospitalization from Covid-19, reducing opportunities for infection is vital. By staying home and getting treatment from a mobile concierge service, you avoid the risk of infection presented by the commute to the doctor’s office and the waiting room.
