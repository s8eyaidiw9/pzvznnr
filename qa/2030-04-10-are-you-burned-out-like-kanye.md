# Are You Burned Out Like Kanye?

After canceling the rest of his tour dates for 2016, rapper Kanye West was hospitalized Monday afternoon. The alleged cause: exhaustion and sleep deprivation, according to PEOPLE.com.

Recently, the 39-year-old music icon has been showing up late to his concerts, leaving the stage early, and pausing performances to go off on tirades and rants.

During one recent rant in California, West revealed he would’ve voted for President-elect Donald Trump, and even dissed his longtime pals Jay-Z and Beyonce.

The bizarre recent events have led many people to wonder: What the heck is going on with Kanye West?

Sources close to the rapper claim his mental health is under control, but they also say he has turned into a workaholic, which has completely worn him out, PEOPLE reports.

And feeling burned out can actually cause you to make stupid decisions, according to a British study.

Researchers found that workers who scored higher on the job burnout scale were more likely to make spontaneous and even irrational choices than people who were happier with their gigs.
