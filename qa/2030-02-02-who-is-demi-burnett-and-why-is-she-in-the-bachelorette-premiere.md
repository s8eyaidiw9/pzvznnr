# Who Is Demi Burnett and Why Is She in 'The Bachelorette' Premiere?

Tonight's episode of The Bachelorette will see Hannah B. get some intel and advice from two of her Bachelor friends, Demi Burnett and Katie Morton. Demi and Katie previously appeared on Colton Underwood's season of The Bachelor with Hannah, and they came back on the ABC show as "Hannah's Angels."

From previews, it looks like one of their main goals will be helping to crack the case of Scott A., the contestant that was accused of having a girlfriend. Fans will love seeing Demi again, and her firecracker personality and no-filter judgements on the guys will make the episode a lot of fun.

But just in case you need a refresher on the self-proclaimed proud country girl, here's what you need to know about Demi Burnett.

Who is Demi Burnett?

Demi is 24 and she's from Red Oak, Texas. According to her ABC bio, "if she could have lunch with anyone, it would be Jon Snow." Before going on The Bachelor, Demi worked as an interior designer in Texas. On the show, Demi opened about her family life, and she told Colton that her mother had been serving time in federal prison. Her mother actually got released while Demi was still on The Bachelor.

What did Demi do on Colton's season of The Bachelor?

Demi's bold actions on the show often drew the ire of the other contestants, and she was a "villain" of sorts—although it has to be noted that fans grew to love her. She was the first one out of the limo on the season's first episode, and in a move that some called clever and others called cringeworthy, she proclaimed that she "hadn't dated a virgin since she was 12."

She also had the first one-on-one conversation with Colton that night, and on the second episode she kissed Colton in front of everyone on the group date. Other memorable antics of Demi's include: playing with the group date rose; giving Colton a massage in her "fantasy closet"; blindfolding and spanking Colton with a paddle; and fighting with fellow contestant Courtney.

On the season's sixth episode, Demi surprised Colton by unexpectedly visiting him after his date with another contestant. As People recapped, Demi opened up about her feelings for him, saying "You give me so much and every moment that I spend with you it leaves me wanting more. I now feel like I am falling in love with you."

In a quite savage move, Colton decided to eliminate her right then and there. "I appreciate you saying that to me. There’s a part of me that just doesn’t know if we can get there.
