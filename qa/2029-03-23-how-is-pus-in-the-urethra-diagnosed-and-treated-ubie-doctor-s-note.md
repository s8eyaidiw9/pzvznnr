Explanation

Pus in the urethra, a condition known as urethral discharge, can be diagnosed by examining the type and nature of the discharge. One method of diagnosis involves observing the characteristics of the pus and understanding its causes, which may not always be due to infection.

For treatment, one approach mentioned is intermittent catheterization. This method involves using a catheter to relieve symptoms and manage the condition, especially in cases related to acute urinary issues. The treatment aims to reduce discomfort and prevent complications. It is important to follow medical advice and seek professional help to ensure proper management of the condition.
