Can meditation or relaxation techniques reduce sleepwalking?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Meditation and relaxation techniques may help reduce sleepwalking episodes. Because stress and anxiety are common triggers for sleepwalking, practices like deep breathing, guided meditation, mindfulness, and progressive muscle relaxation can calm the nervous system, improve sleep quality, and lower the likelihood of nighttime disturbances.

If you or a loved one experiences sleepwalking, it's important to understand the underlying causes—stress, sleep deprivation, medications, or another condition may be at play.
