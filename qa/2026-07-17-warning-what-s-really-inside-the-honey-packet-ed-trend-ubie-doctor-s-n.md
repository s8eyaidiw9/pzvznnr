Reference: Kamath PS, & Wiesner RH. (2001). A model to predict survival in patients with end-stage liver… Hepatology. PMID: 11157951.

People with compromised liver function metabolize medications more slowly, increasing the risk of drug accumulation and toxicity.

Hidden PDE5 inhibitors in honey packets could lead to liver stress, elevated enzymes, or worse in those with cirrhosis.

If you have known liver disease or suspect you do, it's vital to discuss any supplement—even "natural" honey—with your hepatologist or primary care doctor.
