Can dentists help with sleep apnea using oral devices?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, dentists can treat sleep apnea using custom-fitted oral appliances known as mandibular advancement devices (MADs). These mouthpieces gently reposition the lower jaw forward during sleep, helping keep the airway open to reduce snoring and breathing interruptions. Dentists trained in dental sleep medicine often work alongside physicians to diagnose and manage mild to moderate obstructive sleep apnea, offering a comfortable alternative to CPAP therapy.
