Can diet and nutrition influence narcolepsy symptoms?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Diet and nutrition play a key role in managing narcolepsy symptoms. Caffeine may help reduce daytime drowsiness, while fasting or skipping meals can worsen sleepiness. Heavy, high-carb meals often trigger sleep attacks, whereas balanced, protein-rich meals and consistent hydration may support better alertness throughout the day.

If you're struggling with persistent daytime sleepiness, it's important to identify the underlying cause. Narcolepsy shares symptoms with many other conditions, so guessing can delay effective care.
